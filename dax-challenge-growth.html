Did sales grow?

The manager asks one question every month: are we selling more than last month? Write the measure that gives the month-over-month growth, see it match the target, and join the leaderboard.

The brief

  • Growth for a month is (this month’s sales − last month’s sales) ÷ last month’s sales.
  • You get a ready-made measure, [Total Sales], so you don’t need to sum the Amount column yourself.
  • Return a ratio, for example 0.25 for +25%. In Power BI you would format it as a percentage.
  • January compares with December of the previous year. It must also respond to the City filter, and never show a divide-by-zero error.
  • Write one measure. Any approach is welcome: you are graded on the numbers it produces, not on how it looks. It is also tested on a second dataset you can’t see.

Stuck? Two nudges.

Hint 1: how to think about it

You need two numbers: this month’s sales and last month’s sales. [Total Sales] already gives you the first one. The question is how to get the second one.

Hint 2: the tools

CALCULATE lets you change the filter context, and a time-intelligence function such as DATEADD can move the dates back by one month. Store each number in a VAR to keep the formula readable, and use DIVIDE so a zero never causes an error.

The full walkthrough, with several approaches, will be shared on LinkedIn once the challenge closes.
