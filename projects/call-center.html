Operations analytics / Operations project

Make daily operational performance easier to read.

A focused reporting view for contact-center operations, bringing handling time, service level and satisfaction metrics together.

Power BISQLExcel
Business area
Contact-center operations
Focus
AHT, SLA & CSAT
Role

The question

Contact-center teams have to read several indicators together. Handling time, service levels and customer satisfaction each describe a different part of the operation; reviewing them in isolation can hide the wider picture.

The approach

I brought the key operational indicators into a Power BI reporting view, using SQL and Excel in the reporting workflow. The layout supports a consistent daily review rather than a collection of disconnected reports.

Operational KPIs in context

Average handling time, service-level percentage and customer satisfaction are presented as part of the same reporting picture.

A repeatable review

A consolidated dashboard gives operational stakeholders a common place to review performance.

A visible walkthrough

The LinkedIn demonstration adds context to the static dashboard screenshot.

What the report enables

The dashboard organizes operational reporting around the indicators teams use in daily decisions, making it easier to review handling time, service levels and customer satisfaction together.
